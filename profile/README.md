### 🧭 [LocatorBar — The Ultimate Edition](https://github.com/Krylo-60/LocatorBar-Ultimate)
*Advanced, RPG & MMO-style HUD compass and player/waypoint locator bar for Minecraft (1.20 – 26.2)!*
* **Features**: Dynamic degree markings (0°-360°), off-screen edge carets (`◄` / `►`), in-game instant waypoint modal (`B`), Chroma RGB wave & cyberpunk themes, audio packs, and drag-and-drop HUD position editor.
* **Platforms**: Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge, Forge, Paper, Spigot, Purpur, Folia.
